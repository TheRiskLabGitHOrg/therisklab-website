Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share and protect personal information.

Last updated 24 November 2026

The Risk Lab (Pty) Ltd keeps your information confidential, uses it only to do the work you hire us for, and deletes or returns it when the work ends. This policy explains how, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who we are

The Risk Lab (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2026/550124/07, 1008 Courson Street, Val De Vie, Paarl, 7646, South Africa. Our Information Officer is Daniel van der Merwe. Privacy requests: privacy@therisklab.ai.

Two roles

When we handle information about our own clients and website visitors, we are the responsible party. When we handle information a client gives us to do an engagement (for example transaction data, staff lists or documents), we are the operator and act only on the client’s instructions under our contract with them.

What we collect

  • Business contact details of client staff and suppliers: name, work email, phone number, job title.
  • Invoicing details: names, company details and amounts on invoices.
  • Engagement material a client chooses to share: documents, data extracts, meeting recordings and notes.
  • Our website is static. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, company, email address, phone number and message you enter. If you email us, we keep the email.

We do not run marketing, sell information or build profiles. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Why we use it

To perform engagements and contracts, to invoice and be paid, to meet legal and professional duties, and to protect our legitimate interests such as keeping records in case of a dispute.

Who we share it with

Only service providers we need, under written terms: Google (Workspace: email, files, meetings), Anthropic (Claude, our AI assistant), Xero (accounting, invoice details only), and, for running our own systems, Zapier and Meridian (which act on our accounting data), Netlify (hosts our website and receives contact form submissions; no client data), Leaseweb (hosts our diagramming tool in Amsterdam) and ManageEngine (device management: user and device names only). Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit. Client data is not used to train AI models. We share information with authorities only where the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Our providers may process information outside South Africa. Google Workspace data is held in Google’s data centres, which may be outside South Africa; our diagramming tool runs in the Netherlands. We transfer information abroad only where POPIA section 72 allows: the provider is bound by obligations that give substantially similar protection, or you have consented, or the transfer is needed to perform our contract.

How long we keep it

Client engagement data is returned or deleted within 30 days of the engagement ending, unless the client agrees otherwise in writing or the law requires us to keep a record. Invoices and accounting records are kept for the period the law requires. Backup copies are removed through normal backup cycles.

How we protect it

Single sign-on and two-step verification on all systems, access limited to people on the engagement, managed devices, encryption in transit and at rest provided by our platforms, and a written breach procedure. If personal information we hold is compromised, we notify the affected client immediately and in any event within 24 hours of our Data Protection Lead becoming aware, and notify the Information Regulator where POPIA requires.

Your rights

You may ask whether we hold information about you, ask for a copy, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email privacy@therisklab.ai from your business email address. Because we serve business clients, we verify requests against the client’s domain and may refer a request about engagement data to the client, who controls it. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Complaints

Write to privacy@therisklab.ai first. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Visitors from the EU or UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have rights under the GDPR or UK GDPR, including access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Contact privacy@therisklab.ai.

Changes

We review this policy twice a year and publish changes here with the date.
