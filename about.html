The story behind the sturgeon…

Built to endure. Driven to return. Designed to create value.

Long before it became our mark, the sturgeon had already mastered a demanding environment. Its fossil record runs back more than 120 million years, and some species live beyond a century. Built on cartilage rather than bone, much like sharks, it is slow to mature, resilient and made for the long term.

Many sturgeon move between sea and river. At the right time, they turn upstream, navigate resistance and return to freshwater to spawn. Salmon make that journey once and mostly die soon after; sturgeon survive it, move back downstream and return to do it again every few years, across a life measured in decades.

Their roe becomes caviar: something rare and valuable, created through patience, care and disciplined stewardship. Responsible aquaculture can produce this value while reducing reliance on vulnerable wild populations.
