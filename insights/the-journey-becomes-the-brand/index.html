The journey becomes the brand

The fish gives us a disciplined way of describing how we see, think and work.

By Robert Sturgeon · Founder and Lead Consultant · 3-minute read · Published 12 October 2026

Our mark is a sturgeon. We kept it because the way the fish lives maps closely onto the way we believe risk and audit work should be done: patient, protected without being rigid, willing to move against the current, and focused on creating value that lasts.

Here are the six parts of that journey, and what each one means for how we work with clients. For the fish itself, read the story behind the sturgeon.

SEE BELOW THE SURFACE

Find the signals others may miss.

Disciplined attention reveals hidden risk, weak controls and unclear accountability.

LONGEVITY

Think beyond the immediate issue.

Build controls and processes that remain useful as the business grows.

PROTECTIVE SCUTES

Protected, not rigid.

Layered safeguards should create resilience without unnecessary bureaucracy.

THE UPSTREAM JOURNEY

Face difficult issues directly.

Move against complexity to find the risks management most needs to understand.

RETURN AND RENEWAL

Find the root cause. Leave it stronger.

Look beneath symptoms, then support ownership, implementation and closure.

CAVIAR

Create value through discipline.

Turn careful analysis and experienced judgement into something genuinely valuable.
