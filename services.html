SERVICES

Practical risk and control services

We help businesses identify the risks that matter, assess whether key controls are working and resolve gaps in a practical and commercially sensible way. Our services can be delivered as focused projects or as ongoing risk support.

01

Business Process Reviews

Independent, end-to-end reviews of critical business processes to identify control gaps, unclear accountability, revenue leakage, fraud exposure and opportunities to improve how the process operates.

Our reviews focus on the key controls and risks that matter. We avoid overloading management with minor housekeeping issues or unnecessarily complicated reports.

  • Order-to-cash
  • Billing and revenue
  • Procure-to-pay
  • Seller/merchant onboarding and monitoring
  • Seller/merchant disbursements and payments
  • Finance processes
  • Fraud and operational-risk processes
02

Risk Registers and Risk Management Support

Develop or refresh risk registers that give management a clear view of the organisation's most important risks, existing controls, ownership and required actions.

We help management move beyond a static risk document and establish a risk-management process that supports better decision-making.

  • Risk identification workshops
  • Risk-register development
  • Risk and control assessments
  • Risk ownership and accountability
  • Action and remediation tracking
  • Executive and board reporting
  • Regular risk-register updates
  • Ongoing risk-management support
03

Remediation and Control Improvement

Help management respond to identified findings by designing practical controls, clarifying ownership and supporting implementation.

We can assist with issues identified through internal audit, external audit, risk reviews, regulatory assessments or management's own monitoring.

  • Root-cause analysis
  • Remediation planning
  • Control design
  • Procedure development
  • Ownership and accountability
  • Implementation support
  • Progress monitoring
  • Closure validation
04

Ongoing Risk Support

Flexible access to experienced risk support without the cost or commitment of a full-time senior appointment.

This service is suitable for growing businesses, finance teams, risk functions or management teams that need regular support but do not require a large internal risk department.

  • Risk-register maintenance
  • Internal audit remediation
  • Management and board reporting
  • Targeted process reviews
  • Control advice
  • Emerging-risk assessments
  • Policy and procedure reviews
  • Risk workshops
  • Ad hoc management support
05

Risk and Control Training

Straightforward, business-focused training for finance, operations, management and risk teams.

Training is designed to be practical, accessible and directly relevant to the organisation's operating environment.

  • Risk-management fundamentals
  • Risk identification and assessment
  • Control design
  • Fraud awareness
  • AI in risk management
06

AI-Enabled Risk Management

Help businesses identify practical opportunities to use AI to improve risk management, internal audit and control activities.

This is not about adopting technology for its own sake. It is about finding focused applications that improve quality, speed, consistency and insight.

  • Analysing policies and procedures
  • Identifying inconsistencies across documentation
  • Supporting risk identification
  • Reviewing control descriptions
  • Analysing transactional or operational data
  • Identifying exceptions and unusual patterns
  • Tracking remediation actions
  • Preparing risk and audit reporting
  • Supporting continuous monitoring
HOW WE WORK

A focused and practical approach

1

Understand the business

We review the process, documentation, available data and management's perspective to understand how the business actually operates.

2

Identify what matters

We distinguish fundamental risks and control gaps from minor housekeeping issues, focusing management attention where it will have the greatest impact.

3

Develop practical solutions

We provide clear, prioritised recommendations that take account of the organisation's size, resources, systems and commercial realities.

4

Support implementation

Where required, we work alongside management to design controls, clarify actions and support the business through implementation and closure.

OUR APPROACH

Practical support from identification through to resolution

We do not believe a consultant's role should end when a finding is raised. Where appropriate, we work alongside management to:

  • Understand the root cause of the issue
  • Design a practical and proportionate response
  • Clarify ownership and accountability
  • Develop or improve controls
  • Support implementation
  • Track progress through to closure

The objective is not simply to produce a report. It is to leave the business stronger than we found it.

AI AND TECHNOLOGY

Using AI to deliver better risk support

The Risk Lab actively uses AI to improve the way risk and audit work is performed. We use AI-enabled tools to support research, analyse large volumes of information, compare documentation, identify patterns and improve the consistency and efficiency of our work.

This enables us to deliver work faster while maintaining a strong focus on professional judgement, business context and practical outcomes.

We also help clients assess how AI could improve their own risk management activities, including:

  • Risk identification and assessment
  • Control monitoring
  • Data analysis and exception identification
  • Audit and review planning
  • Remediation tracking
  • Policy and procedure reviews
  • Management and board reporting

We believe the future of risk management will combine experienced human judgement with the speed and analytical power of AI.
