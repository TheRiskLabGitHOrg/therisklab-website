HOW WE WORK

Assess. Fix. Maintain.

Three ways we work with you, from the first review to controls that keep working. The objective is not to produce a report. It is to leave the business stronger than we found it. Start wherever you need us: AI is built into each step, and professional judgement and accountability remain human.

  1. 01 · Assess

    See where you’re exposed

    We review your key processes end to end and map the risks that could disrupt the business, so you know what matters and whether your controls work. AI-driven analytics run on complete transaction data, not samples.

    Typical projects
    • End-to-end process reviews
    • Risk register development or refresh
    • Fraud and control gap assessments
  2. 02 · Fix

    Close the gaps

    We trace each issue to its root cause, design the right control and work alongside your team until it is closed. Where manual checks can’t keep up, we build automated and AI-driven controls.

    Typical projects
    • Audit finding remediation
    • Control design and documentation
    • Automated and AI-driven controls
  3. 03 · Maintain

    Keep it fixed

    We stay on as your risk partner, keeping the register current, running continuous monitoring on key controls and reporting to management and the board, without the cost of a full-time senior hire.

    Typical projects
    • Ongoing risk support
    • Continuous control monitoring
    • Management and board reporting
